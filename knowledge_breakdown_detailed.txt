KnowledgeBound: Detailed Knowledge Breakdown
This document explains exactly how every single knowledge category works, how you level it up, what happens when you fail, and the exact step-by-step checks the game makes behind the scenes in layman's terms.

====================================================================
MATERIAL JOBS (5 Tiers) - Cannot jump tiers.
====================================================================

1. Toolsmithing (Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, Hoes)
2. Weaponsmithing (Swords, Bows, Crossbows)
3. Armouring (All Armor)

How it works:
These three jobs govern your ability to craft gear out of better materials (Wood -> Stone -> Iron -> Diamond -> Netherite). 

- How to get XP: Crafting items that belong to that job.
- Step 1 (Before Crafting): When you put the ingredients in the crafting table, the game checks your tier against the item's tier. If you are Tier 1 (Stone) and try to craft a Diamond (Tier 3) chestplate, the game completely blocks you. The item won't even appear in the output slot.
- Step 2 (During Crafting): If you are crafting something at or below your level, the game rolls a dice based on how hard the craft is for you. 
- Step 3 (After Crafting): 
  - Fail: The ingredients vanish, and you get absolutely nothing.
  - Poor: You successfully make the item, but you messed it up. It will only have 10% of its maximum durability.
  - Normal: You craft a perfect, normal item.

====================================================================
GATHERING JOBS (5 Tiers)
====================================================================

4. Forestry (Chopping Wood)
5. Mining (Mining Stone & Ores)
6. Digging (Shoveling Dirt, Sand, Gravel)
7. Farming (Harvesting fully grown crops)

How it works:
These govern your ability to gather raw resources from the world.

- How to get XP: Breaking the correct blocks for that skill.
- Step 1 (Before breaking): The game checks two things: Was this block placed by a player? (If yes, no XP for you, to prevent exploiting). Second, are you using the correct tool tier? (If you are Tier 1 Mining, you MUST use a Stone Pickaxe to gain XP towards Tier 2).
- Step 2 (The Break): You finish breaking the block. The game rolls a dice based on your skill tier. At Tier 0, you have a huge chance to fail (40% for mining, 30% for farming). 
- Step 3 (After breaking): 
  - On Fail: The block breaks, your tool takes damage, but ZERO items drop. The resources are ruined.
  - On Success: The block drops normally.

====================================================================
CLASS JOBS (3 Tiers) - Can attempt items above your level!
====================================================================

8. Carpentry (Crafting Wooden items like Beds, Chests, Barrels)
9. Masonry (Crafting Stone/Brick items, Redstone components, Glass)

How it works:
Unlike Material jobs, you CAN try to craft something above your level, but it's very risky.

- How to get XP: Crafting items in these categories.
- Step 1 (Before Crafting): The game checks the tier of the item. For example, Planks are Tier 0, but a Bed is Tier 2.
- Step 2 (During Crafting): The game compares your tier to the item. If you are a Tier 0 Carpenter making a Tier 2 Bed, it rolls the dice with a massive 85% fail chance. 
- Step 3 (After Crafting):
  - On Fail: Your wood and wool are destroyed. You get nothing.
  - On Poor/Normal: Same as material jobs (but blocks don't have durability, so poor quality usually just acts as a fail for non-damageable items).

10. Stonecutter (Tied to Masonry)
- How to get XP: Using the stonecutter.
- Step 1 (Before use): You MUST be Masonry Tier 1 to even open the stonecutter.
- Step 2 (During use): Whenever you click to cut a stone, the game rolls a chance (10% at Tier 1) that you slip and cut yourself, dealing 1 heart of damage to you!

11. Smelting (Furnaces, Blast Furnaces)
12. Cooking (Smokers, Campfires, Furnaces)

How it works:
These are "Supervised Jobs". You cannot just put iron in a furnace and go mining.

- How to get XP: Successfully completing a smelt/cook.
- Step 1 (During Smelting): Once the furnace starts, the game draws an invisible 16-block circle around it. If you step outside this circle, a hidden "grace period" timer starts counting down (default 30 seconds). 
- Step 2 (If you leave): If you don't run back in time, the furnace fails. The items inside are destroyed, and black smoke particles appear to show it burned up.
- Step 3 (After Smelting): When the item is done, a "collection window" timer starts (default 60 seconds). If you don't take the item out of the furnace before time runs out, the item ruins/burns away.

13. Beekeeping (Honey and Honeycombs)
- How to get XP: Harvesting a full beehive with shears or a bottle.
- Step 1 (Before harvesting): The game checks your tier. If you try to break the hive with a Silk Touch pickaxe to move it, the game stops you unless you are Tier 3 Beekeeping.
- Step 2 (After harvesting): The game rolls a fail chance based on your tier.
  - On Fail: You mess up the harvest. The bees become ANGRY and attack you, even if you put a campfire under the hive!
  - On Success: If using a bottle, higher tiers have a chance to give you "Royal Honey" instead of regular honey, which gives you Regeneration and Saturation when drank.

14. Husbandry (Taming, Breeding, Riding, Milking, Shearing)
- How to get XP: Successfully interacting with animals.
- Step 1 (Before Breeding/Taming): You right-click the animal with food. The game checks if your tier is high enough for that specific animal.
- Step 2 (On Fail): If you aren't skilled enough, the game cancels the breeding. However, the animal STILL eats your food as a penalty, and the animal gets put on a breeding cooldown so you can't just spam click it. 
- Step 3 (While Riding): When riding a horse, the game checks your skill every 5 seconds. If you aren't skilled enough, the horse has a 25% chance to buck and forcibly kick you off its back.

15. Jeweller (Armor Trims, Gems, Delicate Crafting)
- How to get XP: Applying trims or socketing gems.
- Step 1 (Before Smithing): When you put an armor trim in a Smithing Table, the game checks your Jeweller tier. If it's too low, you are blocked from pulling the item out.

16. Slaughtering (Getting loot from Mobs)
- How to get XP: Dissecting corpses.
- Step 1 (Before Killing): If you kill a cow or zombie with a normal sword, there's a 70% chance it drops literally nothing. To get loot reliably, you MUST use a Cleaver weapon, which spawns an inert "Corpse" on the ground instead of dropping items. (Also, if you kill it with a critical hit, the force ruins the body and it won't spawn a corpse).
- Step 2 (Dissection): You right-click the corpse with an axe or cleaver. The game rolls your Slaughtering fail chance (80% at Tier 0). 
- Step 3 (After Dissection):
  - On Fail: You ruin the corpse. It vanishes and drops 1 Rotten Flesh.
  - On Success: The game judges your butchery. A "Poor" cut gives half the normal loot. A "Normal" cut gives standard loot. An "Excellent" cut gives DOUBLE loot. (Using a Cleaver gives you much higher chances for an Excellent cut than an Axe).

====================================================================
COMBAT JOBS (5 Tiers)
====================================================================

17. Melee Combat (Swords)
18. Ranged Combat (Bows, Crossbows)

How it works:
These govern how much damage you deal, and what armor you are allowed to wear.

- How to get XP: Hitting enemies and dealing damage.
- Step 1 (Before Damage): When you hit a zombie, the game checks your combat tier. At Tier 0, it applies a massive penalty, reducing your damage to only 40% of what it should be. As you level up, this penalty goes away until you deal 100% damage at Tier 4.
- Step 2 (Armor Check): Every tick, the game looks at what armor you are wearing, and compares it to your HIGHEST combat tier (Melee or Ranged). If you are Combat Tier 1, you can only wear Chainmail. If you try to equip Iron armor, the game forcefully rips the armor off your body and drops it on the ground.

====================================================================
FISHING (3 Tiers)
====================================================================

19. Fishing
- How to get XP: Catching fish.
- Step 1 (When a fish bites): You click to reel it in. The game rolls a fail chance based on your tier (40% fail at Tier 0).
- Step 2 (After reeling): 
  - On Fail: The bobber comes back to you, the game says "The fish got away...", and you get no fish. (But you do get XP for trying).
  - On Success: You catch the fish normally.
  - Bonus: You can craft special fishing rods via Toolsmithing (Good Rod, Super Rod) that drastically lower this fail chance.
